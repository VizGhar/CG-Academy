The Goal

You just woke up in some strange looking dungeon. Can you solve all of its riddles to get out?

Just play the game in viewer, you will understand the statement later.

  Tasks

All the tasks are encrypted using AES algorithm in ECB mode.
To solve the tasks, you will receive 16Byte long key while playing the game.
I would recommend you to use CyberChef to decipher the tasks. (Don't forget to use AES algorithm, ECB mode, HEX input and RAW output).
So try to get the key to decipher Episode #1 Intro
